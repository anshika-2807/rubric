04 · RECOMMENDATION

My pick: Mettle — with the wedge mark.

"Mettle" carries the same conviction as "SkillProof" without the literalness. It's a single, unusual word — easy domain story, brandable, and the mark scales from a 16px favicon to a t-shirt. Rubric is the strongest dev/college-audience alternative if you want a more tool-like read; Caliber is the safest if you want a name that already lives in workplace vocabulary.
