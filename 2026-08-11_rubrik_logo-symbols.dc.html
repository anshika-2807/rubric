PURE SYMBOLS · NO NAME · NO LETTERS

Six quirky symbols. Just the marks.

Each one is a standalone glyph — nothing inside it spells anything. Pick the vibe; the wordmark sits next to it later, separately.

01 · THUMBPRINT PROOF · YOU
A wobbly fingerprint. Most literal "proof of you" — no two are the same.
02 · WAX SEAL OFFICIAL · QUIRKY
A pressed wax seal that didn't quite stamp evenly. Says signed, sealed, real.
03 · DOG-EAR FOLDED · NOTED
A page with the corner turned down — like marking a receipt you'll come back to.
04 · X MARKS SIGNED · HERE
An X-as-signature. "Sign here, this is real." Reads at any size.
05 · SCRIBBLE AUTOGRAPH
A loopy signature scribble + underline. Every receipt has a signature on it.
06 · COFFEE RING STUDENT · IRL
A coffee-cup ring on the desk. Most "college student studying" of the bunch. The dot is the spill.
